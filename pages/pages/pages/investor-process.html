INVESTOR PROCESS

A clear path from interest to formal consideration.

The portal is designed to collect useful early information without representing an allocation or approval.

01

Explore

Review the opportunity categories and identify the type of project you want to develop.

02

Submit an expression of interest

Provide your company details, project concept, indicative land requirement, investment range and timeline.

03

Initial review

The responsible team may review whether the proposal is sufficiently defined for further discussion and may request additional information.

04

Due diligence & project assessment

Depending on the project, this may involve corporate, financial, technical, planning, environmental and land-related review.

05

Site and commercial discussions

Where appropriate, potential site suitability, infrastructure requirements, commercial terms and responsibilities can be discussed.

06

Formal approvals and agreement

Any land rights, licences, permits, incentives or agreements must be issued through the applicable official authorities and procedures.
