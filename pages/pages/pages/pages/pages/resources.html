Investor pitch checklist

  • Who is the investor and what is the track record?
  • What will be built and for which market?
  • How much land is required?
  • What capital will be invested?
  • What infrastructure is needed?
  • What is the expected construction and operating timeline?
  • What employment, supply-chain or economic activity could the project create?
